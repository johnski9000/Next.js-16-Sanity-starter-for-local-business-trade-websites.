# GBP Posts — Operating Guide

Where the post batches live and how to keep the cadence going.

## Files
- `2026-pilot-month-8-posts.md` — current pilot batch (4 weeks × 2 posts)
- Future batches: `YYYY-MM-batch-N-posts.md`

## GBP post technical limits
- **Body**: max 1,500 chars
- **Preview line**: first sentence is what shows in profile before "Read more" — keep ≤58 chars where possible (≤80 hard max)
- **CTA buttons** (the only clickable element): Book / Order online / Buy / Learn more / Sign up / Call now
- **No clickable inline links** (GBP strips them)
- **No raw URLs** in body
- **No emojis** (recommended for B2B)
- **Image**: 1200×900 (4:3), max 5MB, PNG or JPG
- **Lifespan**: posts persist ~6 months from publication, but recency boosts visibility — weekly cadence is the floor

## Cadence
- 2 posts/week (Monday + Thursday) is the minimum that signals "active business" to Google
- Mondays: educational / local / sector posts — early-week professional audience
- Thursdays: offer / striking-distance / call-to-action — late-week conversion intent
- Skip a week → ranking impact within ~2 weeks

## When to author the next batch
- After the current 8 posts are all scheduled (typically 3-4 weeks in)
- After GBP Insights shows which posts drove views + button clicks → double down on the winning angles in the next batch
- Whenever a new service or city goes live → drop a "What's new" post within 7 days

## Measurement
Check GBP Insights weekly:
- **Post views** — visibility (rises with cadence)
- **Post clicks** (CTA button) — conversion intent
- **Searches** — what queries are surfacing your profile
Posts that drive >10 clicks in their first week → re-use the angle in the next batch.

## Image sources
- Free: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay (curate, don't grab top results — those are over-used)
- AI-generated: Midjourney, DALL-E, Adobe Firefly — be specific about brand, location, mood
- Custom: commission a photographer for office / team / project shots — best for trust + uniqueness
